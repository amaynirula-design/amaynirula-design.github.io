## Where I started

I earned a B.S. in Materials Engineering with a Business Minor from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, graduating with honors and making the Dean’s List twice. I began my career in project management at Otis Elevator Co., where I moved from associate project manager to project manager and led a portfolio worth more than $47 million.

That work taught me to connect customer needs with the realities of engineering, operations, and delivery. It also shaped the customer-first mindset I bring to product decisions.

## Where I’m going

I’m pursuing an MBA at UC Berkeley’s Haas School of Business, where I’m strengthening my product and strategic toolkit. In summer 2026, I applied that perspective as a Product Management Intern on Tanium’s Endpoint Management team, working across cybersecurity and product strategy.

## A global perspective

I’ve lived and worked in India, China, and the United States. Working across cultures has given me a broader perspective that I bring to every team I join. I speak Hindi fluently and conversational Mandarin.
